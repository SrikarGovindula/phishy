India — Official Portals & Helpline Numbers | Quick Reference Sheet

URGENT CYBER / FINANCIAL FRAUD: If money has been lost or an online financial fraud has just occurred, call 1930 immediately and file the complaint on the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.

Quick decision guide
Situation Contact first
Money lost / UPI, card, bank or online financial fraud 1930 immediately + cybercrime.gov.in; then contact your bank/payment provider.
Phishing link, suspicious phone/SMS/WhatsApp/email or cybercrime cybercrime.gov.in / 1930; use Chakshu for suspected fraud communications.
Company / enterprise cyber incident CERT-In: 1800-11-4949 and incident@cert-in.org.in; follow your internal incident-response process.
Banking complaint not resolved by the regulated entity RBI CMS — cms.rbi.org.in / 14448.
Securities / investment grievance SEBI SCORES / 1800 266 7575 or 1800 22 7575.
Insurance grievance Bima Bharosa / 155255 or 1800 4254 732.
Safety reminder: Use only official government/regulator portals shown here. Never share OTPs, PINs, passwords, CVV, UPI PINs or remote-access credentials with a caller claiming to be from a bank, regulator or cyber cell.
Sources verified from official Government of India / regulator websites: I4C National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal, CERT-In, RBI, SEBI and IRDAI. Contact details can change; verify the official portal before relying on a number.
